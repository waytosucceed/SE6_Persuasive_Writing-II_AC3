Writing Activity:

Imagine your school canteen only sells healthy food, which you find bland.
Write an opinion piece on this (either agreeing with the school’s decision or appealing for a change to snacks and fast food).
Your writing is meant to persuade. So, no matter where you stand, plan your writing accordingly.
Word Limit: 250
Try to use any of the following literary devices:

  • Introduction - 50
  • Body 1 - 60-70
  • Body 2 - 60-70
  • Conclusion - 50
